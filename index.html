About

I earned my B.S. in Geospatial Science and Technology at Cal Poly Humboldt, with an Environmental Science and Management concentration and a minor in Geography. I graduated magna cum laude in August 2026.

My experience includes more than 1,000 hours of ArcGIS Pro work, GIS trail mapping and GNSS data collection at the L.W. Schatz Demonstration Tree Farm, remote sensing, and Python scripting. I am seeking opportunities in GIS and environmental mapping.
